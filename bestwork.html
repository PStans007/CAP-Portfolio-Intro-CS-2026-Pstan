-My Best Work-

I believe I had the most fun doing the Unit 3 Project. I love art and video games, so being able to make one really allowed me to flourish from an artist and coding standpoint. Having gotten through the Unit 6 project, the x-y coords became more familiar to me. It allowed me to spend more time on the game elements. I’m very proud because I also included animation in the sprites of the game, and drew all the sprites myself. I also got to add music. The experience overall was immersive which I was excited to see, as well as letting people play my game. Even though it took a while, I found Baker Dash easy to make. I used the lessons of sprite movements and the is touching command to really enhance my game. As well as adding multiple enemies. Although I still believe Baker Dash needs improvements, overall I believe it is a great game. It was enjoyable and fun for my family and friends to play, and that makes me proud of it.
